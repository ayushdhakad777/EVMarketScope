Data story

How to read the EV landscape

Move from broad market patterns to vehicle-level comparisons, then interpret performance in context.

01 / EXPLORE

Filter the vehicle landscape

Start with manufacturer, model year, vehicle category, manufacturing country or market region to narrow the comparison set.

02 / COMPARE

Balance specifications and cost

Compare driving range, battery capacity, charging metrics, price, insurance, warranty and resale estimates together.

03 / INTERPRET

Consider context and trade-offs

Use sales, ratings, safety and estimated CO₂ savings as complementary indicators, not as a universal ranking.

Methodology

Data, definitions & limitations

A transparent view of the dataset and how to interpret its summary metrics.

Dataset coverage

100,000 vehicle records, 37 fields, model years spanning 2018–2026, and 200 manufacturers represented.

Analysis dimensions

Vehicle specifications, battery technology, charging performance, ownership costs, sales, geography and customer ratings.

Interpretation

Summary figures describe the dataset supplied for this project. They are not independently verified real-world market statistics.

Dataset note: Interpret results in the context of the data source and collection methodology. Aggregated sales figures are sums of the dataset's annual_sales_units field.
